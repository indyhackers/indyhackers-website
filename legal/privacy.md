# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

IndyHackers is a volunteer-run community for Indianapolis-area technologists. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We aim to collect only what we need to run the community.

## Information we collect

- **Account information.** If you create an account or sign in with Google, we store your name and email address so we can identify you and, where relevant, grant access to member or board tools.
- **Job postings.** When you submit a job, we store the details you provide (title, company, description, salary range, and how to apply) and your contact email so we can review and follow up on the listing.
- **Newsletter.** If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is stored with our newsletter provider so we can send you issues.
- **Slack invitations.** If you request a Slack invite, we process your email address (and, for spam prevention, your approximate country and a CAPTCHA result) to send the invitation.
- **Usage data.** Like most websites, we collect basic analytics about how pages are used (for example, pages visited and general device/browser information).

## How we use your information

We use the information above to operate the website, review and publish job postings, deliver the newsletter, send Slack invitations, prevent abuse and spam, and understand which parts of the site are useful so we can improve them. We do not sell your personal information.

## Third-party services

We rely on a small number of third parties to run the community. Your information may be processed by them for the purposes described above:

- Google Analytics — website usage measurement
- Google — optional sign-in (OAuth) and the embedded event calendar
- Buttondown — newsletter delivery
- Slack — community chat and invitations
- Givebutter — donations, if you choose to give

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data.

## Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and to measure site usage through Google Analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features (such as staying signed in) may not work as a result.

## Data retention

We keep information for as long as it is needed to run the community — for example, while your account is active or while a job posting is live. You can ask us to remove your account, unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in each issue, or request deletion of information we hold about you.

## Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us. We will do our best to honor reasonable requests.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Email [help@indyhackers.org](mailto:help@indyhackers.org).
